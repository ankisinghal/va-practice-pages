The winner the rule can name

Test ID: Topic: Editorial difficulty: (a guess before reading, not a measured level)
Words: Reading time: about 2 minutes
Source: original fictional passage written for practice, so full text is included.

In an imagined chess tournament, each of three players faces each other once. Ishan beats Bela, Bela beats Karim, and Karim beats Ishan. Each finishes with one win and one loss. The organiser, Sana, is asked to name the strongest player. A spectator points to Ishan's victory over Bela, then Bela's victory over Karim: surely, he says, Ishan belongs above both.

The final result blocks that tidy ladder. A win records what happened in a particular contest; it is not automatically a relation like being taller than someone. If Ishan is taller than Bela and Bela taller than Karim, their heights settle Ishan's position relative to Karim. The three match results do not behave that way. No explanation of playing styles is needed to see the problem: the recorded outcomes already form a circle.

Sana could break the tie by a rule announced before play. Perhaps the player who used the least total time wins the trophy. That rule would yield an official winner if the times differ. It would not turn the circular results into a straight ordering, or show that speed is the same thing as strength. The trophy would answer a question the rule makes decidable, rather than settle every question spectators might ask.

Nor would a different tie-breaking rule necessarily be dishonest. A tournament might reward points earned against other entrants or arrange further games. What matters is stating which achievement the award recognises, and applying the rule consistently. A rule chosen only after the organiser knows whom it favours raises a separate fairness concern. The need to award a prize does not itself justify choosing that prize's recipient first and its rationale afterwards.

Sana writes the three results on the noticeboard before listing the trophy rule. Readers can now distinguish the event's outcome from the procedure used to resolve its tie. Some still want a single name for the strongest player. She leaves that label off the board. The tournament can finish without making its record say more than the games established, and an official decision need not pretend to be a discovery.
